Topic: Visa Extension and Residence Permit Application
Step 1: Contact your Coordinator: Contact your coordinator immediately. Inform them of your current visa's expiration date and how many days are left. CRITICAL WARNING: You must notify them at least 5 to 10 days before your visa expires.
Step 2: Obtain 3 Documents from the School: The official document required for visa application. A document proving you are a student at NPU. A proof of address document. If you live in the university dormitory, this is your dorm address certificate. If you live off-campus, take your rental contract to the local Police Station (派出所) to get the document.
Step 3: Make an Appointment: One day before going, use WeChat to search for "西安出入境" (Xi'an Exit-Entry) to book your time slot. 
Step 4: Where to Go: NPU students usually go to the Xi'an Public Security Bureau Exit-Entry Administration (西安市公安局出入境管理局).
Step 5: What to Bring: Bring the 3 documents approved by your coordinator, PLUS your Admission Letter and your JW202 form.
